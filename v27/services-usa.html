USA compliance

Federal, state and payroll — against the deadlines that apply to you.

US compliance is less about volume than about scope: which states you have triggered, which international forms your ownership creates, and which of them carry penalties that are not proportionate to the tax involved.

01 What's included

The scope.

01 Federal returns Form 1120 or 1120-F, and the international forms foreign ownership brings with it — 5471, 5472 and related disclosures.
02 State and franchise State income tax where you have activity, Delaware franchise tax, annual reports and registered agent maintenance.
03 Sales tax Nexus review against economic and physical thresholds, registration where you have crossed them, and ongoing returns.
04 Payroll Federal and state registration, withholding, quarterly 941s, annual W-2s and 1099 / W-8 handling for contractors abroad.
