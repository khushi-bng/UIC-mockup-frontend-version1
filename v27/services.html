The engagement

One continuous line, not five projects.

An engagement starts wherever you are — pre-incorporation, mid-scramble, or years in with a backlog — and runs the same line: set up, structure, comply, report, grow.

The point of the line is that nothing restarts at a stage boundary. The entity choice is made knowing what it obliges you to file; the filings are done by the people who keep the books; the books feed a close that is already board-ready.

Pricing follows the same shape: a fixed monthly retainer for the recurring scope, one-off project fees for setup and restructuring — in writing, before onboarding.
