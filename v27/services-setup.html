Entity setup

Incorporate in the right order, on both sides.

Most structures that need unwinding later were not wrong — they were sequenced wrong. Setup is where the cheapest decisions and the most expensive mistakes live.

01 What's included

The scope.

01 Entity choice Delaware C-Corp, LLC or a state entity in the USA; Private Limited or LLP in India — chosen against where revenue lands and where the IP will sit.
02 Incorporation and registrations Filing, registered agent, EIN, PAN and TAN, GST where applicable, and the state registrations your activity actually triggers.
03 Banking on both sides Account opening with the documentation each bank expects, in the order that avoids the circular dependency between entity, address and account.
04 Founder and share structure Cap table, share classes and vesting set at inception, so a later round or an ESOP does not require restructuring.

How it runs.

Structure review

Sequence

Order is the product.

The filings are rarely the constraint — banking and attestation are. A US entity can be incorporated in days; an operating account is usually the long pole. We sequence so the slow items start first rather than last.
