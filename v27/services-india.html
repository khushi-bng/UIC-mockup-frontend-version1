The rhythm

Monthly, quarterly, annual — one calendar.

Indian compliance is not difficult so much as relentless: overlapping GST, TDS, ROC and FEMA cycles, each with its own portal and its own penalty for being late. The calendar is the deliverable; the filings are what falls out of it.

03 Questions

Common questions.

Export of services can be zero-rated under GST, but it depends on the place-of-supply test, receipt of convertible foreign exchange and whether you have filed an LUT. Related-party arrangements between a subsidiary and its parent get looked at more closely, which is why the transfer pricing file and the GST position need to agree with each other.

Late fees and interest accrue per return and are generally not negotiable, so the priority is stopping the accrual rather than arguing it. We quantify the exposure first, then file in the order that reduces it fastest.

If there are transactions with associated enterprises abroad, yes — and the threshold is lower than most founders expect. A subsidiary invoicing its US parent for engineering time is a related-party transaction.

A director or authorised signatory does, on the portal, with their own credentials. We prepare, reconcile and stage everything, and we do not hold your signing credentials.
